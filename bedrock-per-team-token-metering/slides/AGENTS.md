# Slides: bedrock-per-team-token-metering

## Story
1. The moment: a session abstract at the AI Engineer conference asked the question out loud (sourced on slide 6), then the State of FinOps 2026 figure: 98% now manage AI spend, up from 31% two years ago (data.finops.org, slide 7).
2. The tension: every team calls a model; nobody can say which team spent the tokens.
3. Why it is hard: one shared role hides the team behind the call; a bill without an owner is a bill nobody can cut.
4. The questions: who spent these tokens; what may a team call; what stops a mistake before it exists; which credentials does the tooling hold; what will the invoice say; how fast do the counts arrive.
5. The answers: an application inference profile per team with cost allocation tags; a role scoped to its own profile; a Pulumi Policies pack at preview; Pulumi ESC OIDC credentials; metrics in minutes vs invoice tags in up to 24 hours; the demo answers the last.
6. The proof: the demo meters real calls per team, denies a cross-team call, blocks two mistakes at preview, trips an alarm.
